**I value DRM-free games and the very idea of GOG.** I supported this store and tried to buy there — because an offer where you buy a game and the offline installer belongs to you is worth nurturing. What is closest to my heart is couch play: two people, one screen, a split image. That is exactly how I wanted to play Dome Keeper.

## That is why I am writing, not just complaining

Dome Keeper launched on Steam and GOG on the same day in September 2022. The free update with co-op, including local split-screen, shipped on **April 13, 2026** on Steam and Xbox. On the day I am writing this (**October 25, 2026**), the newest public version in my GOG edition is still **4.2.3 from April 8, 2025**, and the product page lists no co-op at all. The difference: **195 days**.

| Status as of 2026-10-25 | Steam | GOG |
|---|---|---|
| Version | v5.0.8 (2026-09-03) | 4.2.3 (2025-04-08) |
| Local split-screen | since 2026-04-13 | not listed |
| The Lost Keepers DLC (paid) | available | not offered |

Let me say it plainly: **such a delay is unacceptable to me.** I am not saying who is at fault — I do not know where along the way this update got stuck, and I will not pretend otherwise. What I do know is that I wish GOG well, and I know what I am missing. Both are true at once; I hide neither.

I also know a different scenario than silence. In 2023, Update 2.4 also arrived in GOG late, and back then a developer **explained on the forum** what was holding it up: crashes occurring only in the GOG version, and a wrapper supplied by GOG that was still in beta. He added that such slips would probably be common. That one honest statement made the waiting bearable. Because it is not the delay that hurts most — it is the not knowing.

## My proposal: four messages instead of silence

I do not know GOG's internal processes and I do not claim any are missing. What follows is an editorial suggestion only — what a simple information loop about updates could look like if the store wanted to look after itself in the voices of players like me:

And concretely, what I would love to know about Dome Keeper:

| What I would like to know | How it helps |
|---|---|
| Is the April 13, 2026 update on its way to GOG? | A supporter does not have to guess or refresh the product page. |
| What is holding it up — technical work, a queue, something else? | An explanation alone, as in 2023, makes waiting bearable. |
| When to expect the version — or "we don't know yet"? | An honest "we don't know" builds more trust than silence. |
| Where do you announce status changes? | One standing place ends the search across forums and tabs. |

## I wish you the polish you promise

I want alternatives like GOG to prosper. I want the Preservation Program GOG praises for the classics to have its everyday counterpart for new games. I want you to give players who paid and are waiting a reason to be proud — and I want to play Dome Keeper on a couch with a second person in the edition I bought from you.

That is why my wishes are concrete, not polite: that co-op reaches GOG, that one message explains what is holding it up, and that such a loop of information becomes your norm. And until that happens, one fact remains that these wishes do not erase: 195 days after the update shipped, my GOG edition still has no local co-op. I want to cheer for you — help me do it.

<details>
<summary>What this text does not claim</summary>

- I do not claim the developer abandoned the GOG version, or that the store is at fault — without an official statement the cause remains unknown.
- I do not connect the change of GOG's owner (2025-12-29) with this delay; slips were already happening in 2023, before the change.
- I quote the community sheet's numbers verbatim: 1172 games, 1573 issues, 325 "Missing Updates" — these are different quantities, and I do not call them "the number of neglected games".
- A free update, paid DLC, online multiplayer and local split-screen are four different things; I never merge them.
- The Preservation Program (2024) is about maintaining the classics — it is not a promise of full catalogue freshness.
</details>
