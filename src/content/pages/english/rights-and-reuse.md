## Who owns the material on this blog

The posts and photographs made by Waldemar Wosiński are his works, and the copyright in them is reserved. Copying, adapting and further distributing that material beyond the limits set by law requires his consent.

Some material belongs to other rightholders and is described as such where it appears:

- the PeeWee product photo in the pellet-sifting post comes from the official PeeWee International B.V. gallery; its rights stay with the rightholders,
- the site's code and layout build on the Astroplate theme under the MIT licence (the `LICENSE` file in the blog's repository).

Posts were prepared with the help of AI tools. On that account we claim no exclusive rights beyond what the law provides.

## What you may do without consent

The Polish Copyright Act allows published works to be used within defined limits without the author's consent — quoting excerpts among them — under permitted use. The limits come from the law, not from this page: permitted use must not conflict with the normal exploitation of the work or prejudice the author's legitimate interests.

When you use our material within those limits, name the author (Waldemar Wosiński) and link the original post address on `droga.ww86.eu` where possible.

## Consent and contact

Credit is not consent. Quotation and other uses the law permits need no asking, but anything beyond them — whole posts, modified versions, republishing elsewhere — does. Ask for consent through the [contact](/en/contact) page.

## What this page does not promise

Reserving rights does not automatically stop bots from copying, nor guarantee that every permitted use is credited. It also does not restrict uses the law allows without the author's consent.
